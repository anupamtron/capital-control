🌍 Capital Control: No Dice

Your Cards. Your Moves. Your Empire.

Capital Control: No Dice is a strategy-focused property trading board game where every move is a decision.

Forget rolling dice! Movement is controlled by cards, making planning, resource management, and predicting your opponents essential to victory.

🎮 Game Features

- No Dice, Card-Based Movement: Use movement cards numbered 2–12 and a special wildcard to plan your moves.
- World-Inspired Board: Explore capital cities of major countries and build your property empire.
- Property Trading: Purchase properties, complete colour groups, construct houses and hotels, collect rent, and mortgage assets.
- Local Multiplayer: Enjoy Pass & Play matches with 2–6 players on one device.
- LAN Multiplayer: Play with others connected to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot.
- Computer Opponents: Challenge AI-controlled players.
- Hardcore Mode: Experience a rising economic dead zone with every movement-card reset.
- Team Mode: Form groups and compete against other teams.
- Custom Game Settings: Adjust starting budgets, house and hotel limits, and other gameplay settings.
- Special Spaces: Encounter taxes, jail, transport routes, utilities, Fortune Chest, and Mystery Chest.

🧠 Multiple Strategies. One Goal.

Will you become a Runner, collecting money from GO? A Camper, carefully saving movement cards to trap opponents? Or a Stealth Investor, saving your resources and building an empire when others are struggling financially?

Every player can develop a different strategy. Your decisions shape the game!

📖 How to Play

Learn the rules, understand movement cards, discover property mechanics, and master the strategies needed to win.

📱 Multiplayer Modes

Local Multiplayer: Pass & Play with friends and family.

LAN Multiplayer: Connect through the same local network.
